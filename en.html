Integrating the worlds of science, consciousness and physical development

The knowledge has existed for hundreds and thousands of years. The tools available to us today let us connect neuroscience, the study of the psyche and consciousness, and physical development - reaching new levels of growth and healing.

The Brain & Nervous System

How automatic negative patterns are built, and how the brain and nervous system can be retrained. We draw on neuroscience and neuroplasticity to understand the mechanisms of stress, habit and reactivity - and to replace automatic survival with patterns of regulation, growth and development.

The Psyche & Consciousness

Beneath every behavior there is a story we told ourselves, and a consciousness that interprets reality. Here we meet the emotional patterns that formed to protect us, and learn to recognize when they no longer serve. Not endless analysis of the past, but precise work with the present: emotion, interpretation and choice.

Physical & Embodied Development

Change doesn't stay in the head - it moves into the body and into life itself. The inner shapes the outer, and the outer shapes the inner: a healthier, stronger body, renewed energy, and personal goals that take form and grow. The body and the physical world aren't a backdrop to the process - they're an inseparable part of it.

The Integration

None of the three worlds is enough on its own. Science without practice stays knowledge; awareness without action stays insight; and movement without understanding stays empty training. SHIFT unites the three into one path: understanding the mechanism in the brain and nervous system, meeting the psyche and consciousness, and anchoring change in the body, the environment and life itself - where inner and outer move together.

A taste of Day 1

Research-based · Stanford 2023

The “inner calm button” - the double sigh

  1. A deep inhale through the nose.
  2. Immediately after, a second short inhale through the nose (“fill all the way up”).
  3. A long, slow exhale through the mouth, until the air is almost fully released.

The technique was tested at Stanford: Balban et al., 2023, Cell Reports Medicine. In the study it was practiced about 5 minutes a day for a month, and improvements in mood and anxiety symptoms were measured. Here it's a short taste - 1-3 rounds for immediate calm.

Three rounds · less than a minute

The Program

The 21-Day Program

Three weeks. One daily step. A guided digital process that begins with getting to know your system — and ends with a creation plan of your own. Here it is, from the inside.

Week 1 of 3

Week One — Awareness and Getting to Know the System

The first week is the foundation of everything.

Before we change habits, beliefs or patterns — it helps to understand what's happening in the body. It appears that a significant part of the mechanisms of the nervous system and the brain evolved over the course of evolution to keep us alive — not necessarily to make us happy.

This week we'll get to know the nervous system: the survival state and the creative state, the hormones that affect us, our personal pattern under stress, and we'll learn a first regulation tool. This is the map from which every later change will be drawn.

Week 2 of 3

Week Two — Changing the System

The first week was about recognition. The second week is about change.

The brain is considered “plastic” — that is, capable of changing. Studies suggest that patterns built through repetition can, it seems, also change through repetition.

This week we'll understand how beliefs and environment may influence biology, what role dopamine plays in craving and habits, why change is so hard — and how it's possible nonetheless.

Week 3 of 3

Week Three — From the Body into Life, and a Direction Forward

You've reached the final week — and you're already not the same person who started.

So far we've understood the nervous system and begun to change it from within. This week we take that into life itself: we'll understand the chemistry of desire (dopamine), strengthen the physical foundation — movement and nutrition, examine who and what nourishes us in our environment, gently confront one fear, and build a routine that holds.

And at the end — a 90-day plan and a look back over the whole journey. Not goals born of fear, but a direction born of the creative state. These final moments are not the end — they're the point of takeoff. You're right here, and you deserve to be proud.

Out of the pain - a breakthrough was born

Our own need to climb out of a deep crisis led us to dive into our inner systems, gathering information and principles from neuroscience, epigenetics and various therapeutic worlds. We went through a strong, lasting change and realized we had to share it with as many people as possible - and from that vision SHIFT was born: an ecosystem that makes accessible a practical journey from automatic reactivity and negative patterns to a life of choice, creation and growth, through learning about the human systems and the scientific and psychological principles behind them.

We don't teach from a place of perfection - but from the path we've walked ourselves, and the one we're still walking.
SHIFT founders

Itay Braverman

Co-founder · Former combat soldier and commander · Fitness coach · NLP coach

For years there was a huge gap between the reality I pictured in my head and the reality I actually lived. Limiting beliefs and destructive patterns followed me, buried deep and beyond my awareness. After my service it all surfaced at once, and within the great pain came a desire to get to know my system and understand how to work with it better. Over time a big shift happened in my life: the positive patterns took command and grew stronger, and the gap between imagination and reality closed completely. I realized I wanted to spread this light to as many people as possible - and there, together with Or Meir, SHIFT was born.

Or Meir Cohen

Co-founder · Former combat soldier and commander, law student.

I didn't learn the shift from survival to creation in a book. After the war I found myself facing a nervous system that stayed on high alert even once the fighting was over, and the search for a way out became what SHIFT teaches today.

  • The architecture of change — key principles from neuroscience, behavior and epigenetics for changing automatic patterns.
